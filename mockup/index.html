Tone circle

Click two notes to swap them, or type a row of 12 distinct notes. Numbered badges and the dashed arrow show the note order of the selected shape.

Split

Groups take every Nth note of the circle (Jarzombek: two 6-note, three 4-note, four 3-note groups). Notes are listed in the shape's play order.

Rhythm (1 bar = 16 sixteenth notes)

Order Rhythm

Lanes are played in the order shown: use the arrows to reorder (or Alt+Up/Down on a block). Drag in a lane to draw a note (start + length), drag a block to move it, its right edge to resize, double-click to delete. Keyboard: arrows move, Shift+arrows resize, Delete removes.

Arrangement

Everything stays on this computer: autosaved in the browser, config files are plain JSON you can edit and share.
